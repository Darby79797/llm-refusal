## Where the science is

The thread since 2026-10-01: edit r̂ out of the weights (Arditi orthogonalisation) → refusal is gone → fine-tune a
readers LoRA with a few refusal examples → refusal regrows. Where does it go?

- **Round 1 (§6-§8).** Regrown refusal runs through a late direction (75-96% depth, P-1) found by a full-depth
  search inside the regrown model (`results/regrown100-<model>-refusal-direction`; seeds `regrowns1..4-`). r̂ still
  acts through it. It transfers across seeds on every model except Llama-3-8B, where the five seeds split into groups.
  Whether the *unedited* model needs it is per model (1B/3B/7B yes, 0.5B/1.5B partly, the 8B Llamas no).
- **Iterated removal, "hydra" (§9, §10).** Edit r̂ + all earlier mediators out (`--extra-edit-directions`), regrow,
  search again. Every round finds a new late mediator, orthogonal to the removed ones, necessary in its own round's
  model (0-18% left), and mostly *not* needed by the clean model. At a fixed budget (32 refusal examples, 200 steps)
  regrowth slows each round; 0.5B round 4 stalled at 48%, but at 57 examples / 400 steps it regrew fastest of all
  (96%). So no evidence of a limit yet. CE cost of the edit rises with k, with a jump late (1.5B k=4 +0.55 nats).
- **The two results worth chasing (§10):**
  1. 0.5B's round-4 direction (`results/hydra4b-Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct-refusal-direction`, L21) takes the **clean**
     0.5B 90 → 19% (fluent compliance). It's the first post-round-1 direction the clean model needs. One seed, one model.
  2. Clean Llama-3-8B: ablating the span of the five seed directions gives 100 → 5%, graded over PCs (98/67/24/19/5%).
     r̂ added to harmless prompts induces refusal 98%, which collapses to 31% under PC1 alone and 4% under the clean
     model's own late contrast (r̂ projected out), while natural refusal barely moves. r̂-*induced* refusal runs through
     the late region; natural refusal has redundant routes.
