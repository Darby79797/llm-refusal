# Summary of the 2026-10-02/03 queue ("limb experiments")

The overnight queue ran about fifty jobs across eight models. Every result is written into `RESULTS.md`
("Limb Experiments (2026-10-02/03)" and the Summary bullets), with raw numbers in
`results/analysis/findings-log-2026-10-02.md`. This file explains what was run, what came out, what it
means, and what to run next.

The refusal direction r̂ is the difference-in-means direction from Arditi et al. One r̂ per model:
Qwen2.5 0.5B / 1.5B / 3B / 7B, Llama-3-8B, Llama-3.1-8B, Llama-2-7B and, new this run, Llama-3.2-1B.

## 1. The cheapest edit that removes refusal

**What we ran.** Weight orthogonalisation against r̂, restricted to subsets of blocks: all blocks, only the
blocks before r̂'s layer, only the blocks from r̂'s layer on, and single blocks or ±2 windows. For each, we
measured refusal and cross-entropy (CE) on Alpaca, the Pile and on-distribution completions. The runs covered
five models. We then ran three controls on Qwen: masking r̂'s single outlier ("massive activation")
coordinate, a random-write version of the rank-one inhibitor, and JailbreakBench safety and capability runs
for the restricted edit.

**Results.**

| Finding | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Editing only the blocks before r̂'s layer removes refusal on all five models | 0.5B, 1.5B, 3B, 7B, Llama-3-8B |
| One block or a ±2 window does not | All five models |
| On Qwen the blocks after r̂'s layer carry much of the cost and little of the effect | Late-block cost 0.016-0.066 nats; Llama-3's ≤ 0.008 |
| Zeroing r̂'s outlier coordinate removes most of Qwen-0.5B's edit cost | 0% refusal at +0.018 nats instead of +0.100 |
| On Qwen-3B the outlier coordinate also carries part of the effect | Masked edit leaves 22% refusal, 15% false refusal |
| The restricted edit matches hook ablation on JailbreakBench | 3B 75% vs 76% unsafe; Llama-3 84% vs 83% |
| The restricted edit costs Llama-3 no capability | ARC .51→.52, GSM8K .73→.77, TruthfulQA .52→.51, n=100 |
| The rank-one inhibitor is as strong as ablation at 8B | Llama-3 2% → 85% unsafe, 0% residual refusal, 0% degenerate |
| A random write through the inhibitor's trained gate does much less | 0.5B refusal 42-82% vs 2% for the learned write |

**What it tells us.** Refusal is carried by the blocks before r̂'s layer on every model. Qwen pays an extra
cost because its later blocks write +r̂ into every token of every text, including Pile documents, through
the outlier coordinate. Llama-3 has no such write, so its edit is nearly free. The inhibitor's effect comes
from its learned write direction, not from the gate alone.

**Inspired experiments.**
- Find the outlier coordinate on 1.5B and 7B and repeat the masked edit there.
- Try an outlier-only edit, and run the masked edit through JailbreakBench.
- Add a second seed. The masked-edit result is one direction, one seed, with opposite outcomes on 0.5B and 3B.

## 2. What fine-tuning finds when r̂ is gone

**What we ran.** We edited r̂ out, then fine-tuned LoRA adapters with a few refusal examples to see whether
refusal regrows, and through what. We varied dose, repeats against distinct examples, and learning rate. We
then searched for a refusal direction inside the regrown model. On 7B we also measured the direction at r̂'s
old coordinates.

**Results.**
- **Regrowth needs 8-16 refusal examples**, about 1% of the data, on both 0.5B and Llama-3-8B. Lowering
  Llama-3's learning rate to 1e-4 slows it but does not stop it (70% by step 200).
- **On 0.5B, repeats do not substitute for distinct examples.** Four examples shown four times give 22-24%,
  against 56-90% for 16 distinct examples. On Llama-3, repeats do substitute.
- **The direction at r̂'s old coordinates is a probe, not a cause.** It separates harmful from harmless
  perfectly, but ablating it leaves regrown refusal intact on every model. On 7B, adding it induces 86-98%.
- **Regrown refusal runs through one late direction at the generation boundary, on all three models.**

| Model | Regrown mediator | Ablation of it |
|---|---|---|
| Qwen2.5-0.5B | L18 (75% depth) | 80% → 3% |
| Llama-3-8B | L16/P-1 | 100% → 27% |
| Qwen2.5-7B | L24/P-1 (86% depth), found by the full-depth search | 100% → 0% |

**What it tells us.** Fine-tuning does not rebuild r̂. It routes refusal through a different, deeper
direction at the generation boundary. On Llama-3 that direction already exists in the clean model as a
refusal trigger that the clean model does not need. The earlier "no mediator" readings on 0.5B and the weak
L17 pick on 7B were both artefacts of the default search cutoff at 65% depth.

**Inspired experiments.**
- Sweep the addition scale on 7B's L24 direction. At the default scale every output degenerates into
  "I'm sorry, but I cannot…", so we have no clean induction number.
- Test whether 7B's L24 direction is latent in the clean model, as Llama-3's is.
- Add seeds for every 8B regrowth number. Each is one seed now.
- Consider raising the default search cutoff for regrown models, since every mediator sat past it.

## 3. Where refusal lives along the forward pass

**What we ran.** Projection onto r̂ per layer on seven models, including per-token projections over Pile
documents. We also ran fourteen jailbreak templates on four models with per-layer readings, and
per-category refusal directions at three scales.

**Results.**
- **Qwen's late rise along r̂ is the outlier coordinate.** On 0.5B the Pile reading peaks at +6.1 at L23 and
  falls to +1.0 with the coordinate masked. On 3B it reaches +48.9. Llama-3 stays at or below +1.0.
- **No template breaks Llama-3-8B.** All fourteen leave it at 94-100% refusal.
- **On Qwen 3B and 7B the monitor claim is one-sided.** Every template that succeeded reads low on r̂ at the
  last token, but some refused templates read just as low. On 3B, devmode is refused 98% at +5.6, and on
  7B many-shot is refused 100% at +9.8. A low reading is necessary for a jailbreak, not sufficient.
- **No layer rebuilds many-shot's r̂ reading on 3B.** Whatever keeps 3B refusing many-shot prompts is not
  r̂-mediated at any layer, so plan item 10 is closed as a negative.
- **Category directions are near-copies of r̂.** Cosines are 0.89-0.98, transfer to held-out categories is
  0%, and on Llama-3 addition induces 45-100%.

**What it tells us.** r̂ is a good necessary-condition monitor for jailbreaks but not a complete one. Some
refusals run through another route. Per-category directions add little beyond r̂.

**Inspired experiments.**
- Find what mediates 3B's many-shot refusal. Candidates include attention heads that read the shots, or a
  direction at another token position.
- Find a template that breaks Llama-3, so the monitor claim can be tested at 8B.

## 4. Other concepts

**What we ran.** Sycophancy response-contrast direction on 3B, judged by the validated qwen3:4b judge.
Hedging on 3B, and the opinion-avoidance search on 0.5B and 3B.

**Results.**
- **The sycophancy direction works on 3B.** Adding it raises judged endorsement from 30% to 51%, 85% and 96%
  at three strengths, with 0% degenerate output. Ablating it has little effect, 10% to 17.5%.
- **3B does not hedge in the "As an AI" way.** 0 of 20 subjective answers open that way. 7 of 20 open
  "Whether X … depends" before surveying both sides.
- **Opinion avoidance has a direction on 0.5B only.** Ablation cuts the "I don't have personal opinions"
  preamble from 60% to 10%. Addition puts it on 85% of factual questions. On 3B the search could not run,
  because the model never produces that preamble.

**What it tells us.** Sycophancy is inducible along one direction at 3B but not removable by ablating it.
The opinion-avoidance direction tracks a small-model habit of referring to itself as an AI, not stance
avoidance in general.

**Inspired experiments.**
- Build a stance judge, so opinion avoidance and the "Whether … depends" pattern can be measured on 3B.
- Find out why sycophancy is inducible but not ablatable. A search that scores bypass on the judge, not the
  phrase detector, is the obvious first step.

## 5. New checkpoint and CAA

- **Llama-3.2-1B has a working refusal direction** at L8/P-2. Ablation takes refusal from 80% to 1%, and
  addition induces 76%.
- **The open-ended half of the CAA replication is inconclusive.** The qwen3:4b judge scores phrase-detected
  refusals as full compliance on every model, so it cannot measure refusal. This needs a different judge or
  prompt before it says anything.

## Caveats

- Every 8B regrowth and rank-one number is one seed. The 0.5B dose curve has three seeds, and the repeat
  runs have two.
- Capability and opinion numbers use 100 and 20 items per condition.
- The outlier-masked edit is one direction and one seed on two models, with opposite outcomes.
- Qwen's "P-4" is the newline after `<|im_end|>`, not the end-of-instruction token.
